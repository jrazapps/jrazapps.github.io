Privacy Policy – Whisker Blocks

Effective date: November 24, 2026

This privacy policy explains how Whisker Blocks (the “Game”) handles information. The Game is made and published by Jeremy Razmus (JRaz Apps), an individual developer (“I”, “me”, “we”). Questions? Email jrazapps@gmail.com.

The short version
  • There are no accounts. You never give us your name, email or password to play.
  • Your game progress (scores, coins, settings, unlocked themes) is stored only on your device.
  • The Game shows ads using Google AdMob, which collects some device information (such as your advertising ID and IP address) to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud.
  • There are no in-app purchases. Everything in the Game is unlocked by playing.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we don’t collect any ourselves.

1. Information stored on your device only

The Game saves the following on your phone so it can work:

  • High score, Daily Challenge progress and game statistics
  • Coin balance and items you unlocked (themes)
  • Settings (sound, haptics) and your ad privacy choices

This information stays on your device and is not sent to us. If you uninstall the Game or clear its data, it is deleted.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob), provided by Google LLC, to show banner ads, between-game ads and optional reward videos. When an ad is requested, the AdMob SDK may automatically collect and send to Google:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID (or Apple’s IDFA, only if you allow tracking on iPhone) and the app set ID
  • IP address, which can be used to estimate your general (approximate) location, such as your country or city. The Game does not ask for or use your precise GPS location.
  • App interactions, such as app launches, taps and ad/video views
  • Diagnostic information, such as app performance, launch time and crash/hang data

Google uses this information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. See Google’s documentation: AdMob data disclosure, How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices about ads

  • Personalized ads: On Android, open Settings › Privacy › Ads (wording varies by phone) to delete or reset your advertising ID; ads then become non-personalized. On iPhone, the Game asks permission before any tracking; you can say no and change it later in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Europe, UK and Switzerland: When required, the Game shows a consent message (Google’s User Messaging Platform) the first time you open it.
  • Ad privacy choices: You can review or change your ad consent at any time from the Game’s Settings › Ad privacy choices.
  • Reward videos are always optional and only play if you tap them.

3. What we do NOT do

  • We do not have user accounts or log-ins.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data.
  • We do not access your precise location.
  • We do not sell your personal information to anyone.
  • We do not run our own analytics or tracking servers.
  • We do not sell anything in the Game and do not process payments.

4. Children

The Game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has used the Game in a way that caused personal information to be collected, please email jrazapps@gmail.com and we will help.

5. Your privacy rights (US state laws, GDPR and others)

Depending on where you live (for example California and other US states, the EEA, the UK or Switzerland), you may have the right to know what personal information is processed about you, to access or delete it, to object to or limit certain processing, and to opt out of personalized (“targeted” / “cross-context behavioral”) advertising.

Because we do not collect or store personal information ourselves, most requests are best handled on your device and with Google:

  • Opt out of personalized ads by deleting/resetting your advertising ID (see section 2) or through Settings › Ad privacy choices in the Game.
  • Delete all game data by uninstalling the Game or clearing its storage in your phone’s settings.
  • For data held by Google, see policies.google.com/privacy and myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy.

Some US state laws may treat allowing an ad network to use device data for personalized ads as “sharing” or “targeted advertising”. We do not sell personal information for money, and you can opt out of personalized ads at any time as described above. You can also contact us at jrazapps@gmail.com with any request; we will respond within the time required by law and will not treat you differently for exercising your rights.

Legal basis (EEA/UK users): ads are shown on the basis of your consent where consent is required, and otherwise on the basis of our legitimate interest in funding a free game. Google may transfer data outside your country under its own safeguards.

6. Data security and retention

We do not keep a copy of your data. Data sent by the AdMob SDK is encrypted in transit (TLS) and retained by Google according to Google’s policies. Data stored on your device remains there until you delete the Game or its data.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the “Effective date” above. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the Game’s update notes.
